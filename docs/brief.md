# Project Brief: UniPact Wedding Photography Portfolio

## 1. Executive Summary
UniPact Photography is a premier wedding photography brand dedicated to capturing unscripted romance, timeless emotion, and cinematic elegance for discerning couples worldwide.

## 2. Core Objectives
1. Deliver an immediate **"Amazed" emotional impact within 5 seconds** of landing through dark obsidian luxury aesthetics and ambient cinematic movement.
2. Structure imagery into **curated emotional chapters** (*The Anticipation*, *The Sacred Vows*, *Intimate Portraits*, *After Dark*) with full-screen lightbox inspection and camera provenance.
3. Demystify investment with **3 transparent, beautifully curated packages** and a luxury add-ons menu.
4. Streamline bookings using a boutique **"Tell Me Your Story" questionnaire** designed to elicit personal connection and pre-qualify inquiries.

## 3. Tech Stack
React (Vite), Modular Vanilla CSS design system, Lucide icons, Google Fonts (Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, Plus Jakarta Sans).
