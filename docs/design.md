# Design System & UI Specification: UniPact Portfolio

## 1. Aesthetic Direction: Cinematic & Midnight Noir
The visual language is crafted to feel like an evening exhibition in an exclusive contemporary art gallery—restrained, deeply emotional, and luxurious.

## 2. Color Palette & Tokens
- `--bg-base`: `#09090c` (Obsidian canvas)
- `--bg-surface`: `#121217` (Card backgrounds, modals)
- `--bg-surface-elevated`: `#1a1a24` (Hovered cards, popups)
- `--accent-gold`: `#d4af37` (Primary warm brushed gold)
- `--accent-gold-glow`: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)`
- `--accent-champagne`: `#f5e6c8` (Champagne highlight)
- `--text-primary`: `#f8f8fa` (Crisp ivory headings)
- `--text-secondary`: `#9ea0b2` (Muted silver body copy)
- `--border-subtle`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- `--border-gold`: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`

## 3. Typography
- **Display Headings**: `Cormorant Garamond` & `Playfair Display` (Google Fonts)
- **Body & UI**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Inter` (Google Fonts)

## 4. Key Components
- Glassmorphic Header & Mobile Nav Drawer
- Cinematic Hero with slow-motion Ken Burns ambient effect & accolades
- Emotion-driven Filterable Gallery Tabs (Anticipation, Sacred Vows, Portraits, After Dark)
- Full-Screen Lightbox with EXIF camera metadata and keyboard controls
- Investment Tiers (Elopement, Full Story, Destination) + Add-ons menu
- "Behind the Lens" philosophy + authentic couple testimonials
- "Tell Me Your Story" guided questionnaire with package pre-selection & celebration state
